*Outside the Boxplot* is a digital garden for thinking out loud about data communication.

I work at the intersection of data and storytelling — helping people understand what numbers mean, why it matters, and how to make that meaning legible to others. That means a lot of time thinking about charts, words, structure, and the gap between insight and communication.
